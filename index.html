HOW TO PLAY AMPE

Ampe is a Ghanaian game of rhythm, timing, and quick movement. Players jump and clap as they reveal a foot. This digital circle keeps the simultaneous foot choice and team contest, with the server resolving every exchange for all connected players.

SYNC / OPAPA

When both feet match, the SYNC team wins the exchange, whether its player is attacking or defending.

SWITCH / OSHESHE

When feet differ, the SWITCH team wins. In Team Elimination, the winner attacks the earliest surviving player on the other side.
